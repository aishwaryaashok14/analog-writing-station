# Fountain pen recording

`fountain-pen.mp3` is adapted from **Fountainpen_Write_1_Texture** by **hanasmusic**.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/hanasmusic/sounds/841413/
- Audio: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/841/841413_10563897-hq.mp3
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source description: fountain pen writing on notebook paper, recorded with an AKG C414 XLII microphone.
- Edits: converted to mono, removed rumble below 120 Hz, increased level by 8 dB, added short edge fades, encoded as 128 kbps MP3.

The app uses short fades and different starting points in this recording, playing only while the nib is writing. No synthetic noise is mixed into the writing sound.
